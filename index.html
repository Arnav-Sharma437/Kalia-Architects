MORE THAN JUST A BUILDING.

Architecture has the ability to influence how we live, work and connect. Good architecture brings together structure, interiors, and landscape to create coherent environments that respond to their surroundings and the people who inhabit them.

Led by founder Ram Kumar Kalia, Kalia Architects brings over 30 years of architectural practice based in the Himalayan region of Dharamshala and Kangra. Our projects celebrate local materials, site ecology, and context, translating the unique mountain character into enduring structures of quiet confidence.

PHILOSOPHY

A CONSIDERED APPROACH TO ARCHITECTURE

Every project begins with understanding its context, its purpose, and the people it serves.

We translate these insights into architecture through thoughtful planning, restrained material palettes, natural light, and carefully considered details—creating spaces that feel effortless, enduring, and distinctly their own. Our architecture is designed to withstand the tests of time and mountain weather, aging gracefully into the surroundings.
